## Constraints

- The design document owns L1-L13 and the phase order; this plan owns implementation assignments and
  evidence (`archive_docs/2026-10-28/2026-10-15_link_model_plan.md:485-514`; rulings at
  `technical_architecture/2026-10-15_link_model.md:171-241`). Binding on what is left:
  - L1: a link is a derived reading keyed by (stratum, unordered endpoints), never stored or synced;
    a link that needs identity is promoted by freeze. L2: a withdrawn assertion stays hidden in its
    bucket until its predicate's lifecycle compacts it locally or an accepted checkpoint does, when
    shared; erasure is a separate act. L5: default SPARQL, export and held readers see held
    assertions only; withdrawn ones appear through an explicit history scope.
  - L4: a closed link is one stroke, at most two per surface pair, one per stratum; direction and
    opposition are marks, never extra lines; the fan is retired.
  - L7: record time is the only axis; asserter-claimed valid time, when a consumer needs it, is one
    optional interval in `basis` and never the timeline's default axis. L9: every held assertion
    contributes 1 to strength; traversal count and recency do not feed it.
  - L12: content predicates keep withdrawals until a checkpoint, session and arrangement predicates
    drop them at session end, Traversal compacts into its rollup. L13: two independent reasons from
    one asserter are two separately withdrawable assertions, keyed by method IRI without version
    plus sorted evidence references.
- Preserve separately attributed assertions and derive the pair link; an assertion's RDF reifier
  gives addressability without a stored pair entity (`:485-492`).
- Responsive paths start disabled; view-local controls enable field response and compatible-link
  attraction independently; relationship membership alone never installs a force; temporary path
  geometry keeps the canonical link and stratum, the displayed occurrence and the exact directed
  assertion correspondence; no path point, compatibility group or temporary field is saved as source
  truth (`:60-86`).
- Keep fixture, package, host, native and persistence claims separate; a green unit suite is not
  product acceptance. The documentation-audit baseline debt (uncovered documents, a snapshot digest
  mismatch, broken links, missing known-root paths, stale annotations, unheaded sources) is not a
  passing global gate (`:572-585`).

## Phases

### M1. Responsive-path legibility and cohort limits
The ordinary 2D square-node cohort is qualified, and the shared billboard model covers tilted,
rounded, scaled and lifted profiles. Open, each keeping its named bound until a continuation
qualifies it: close crossings can still crowd marks; larger-cohort and zoom qualification; quiet
unselected links keep the existing grammar; conservative overlap refusal, central crossing density
and grammar-versus-territory contrast; scripted-profile and visible-stem limits; region and
scalar-field response, animated field clocks, larger-cohort performance and persistent response
settings; the region-inspection fixture that stalled before its first presented frame in two browser
processes ("open in its own lane"); the narrow inspector scrolls long records and its font lacks
direction-arrow glyphs; Resource owner lookup scans retained data on a click, with no budget claim.
